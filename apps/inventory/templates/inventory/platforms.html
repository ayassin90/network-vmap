Reading the table

  • Supported: built and tested for this platform.
  • Via SNMP: works through the standard MIBs (IF-MIB, LLDP-MIB, IP-MIB). Results depend on what the vendor implements; add an SNMP credential to the device.
  • Manual: the vendor does not offer a reliable remote way (for example web-UI-only firmware). VMap tracks the version and PSIRT exposure, but you upgrade by hand.
  • Planned: on the roadmap. Tell us which vendor you need next and it moves up the list.
